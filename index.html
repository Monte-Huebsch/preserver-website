What is Preserver?

If you're searching for the meaning of "preserver" as a life-saving flotation device, or as something used in food preservation, this isn't that.

Preserver is a mobile app for iOS and Android that turns your phone into a real-world data collector. It captures photos, video, voice recordings and text notes, and automatically stamps every one with the exact time, date and GPS location — creating a verifiable record of when and where something happened.

Then it does what no camera roll can: it exports everything as a structured, AI-ready bundle — media, transcripts, locations, timestamps and integrity hashes in machine-readable format. Drop the bundle into ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Grok or NotebookLM and turn a day in the field into a finished report in minutes. Capture is free, works offline, and needs no registration.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Preserver free?
Capturing with Preserver is completely free — unlimited captures, full GPS and timestamp metadata, offline operation, no registration. AI-ready export bundles are also free during our beta, and everyone who uses them during the beta keeps them free — you're grandfathered in. In future, new users will pay a small monthly charge for the Pro version. You never pay to capture or to access your own data.
Does it work without an internet connection?
Absolutely. Preserver works completely offline — no Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, or mobile data required. Your captures are stored on your device until you choose to back them up or share them.
Where is my data stored?
On your device, by default. If you choose, you can store your Activities in your personal cloud account (Google Drive, iCloud, Dropbox, etc.). You're always in control.
Do you share my data with third parties?
Never. Preserver does not sell your data or use it to train AI models. Your captures stay on your device or in your own cloud. If you export an AI-ready bundle, you choose which AI service receives it — under your own account.
