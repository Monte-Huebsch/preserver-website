    Who Uses Preserver?

    From tradies to researchers, journalists to families — anyone who needs to capture what happened, exactly when and where it happened, and turn it into finished reports with AI.

    Last updated September 2026

    Every use case below ends the same way: capture in the field, export a structured AI-ready bundle — timestamps, GPS, transcripts, integrity hashes — and let ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini or Grok write the report, log or summary from verified data. AI exports are free during beta.
